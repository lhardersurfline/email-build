Cat 5 Hurricane Genevieve Sends SoCal Solid Tropical Swell
Hurricane Genevieve has weakened in the past 24 hours but is still a large and dangerous storm. Cabo and southernmost Baja are feeling major impacts from the tropical cyclone today in the form of XL surf, very strong wind, and rain. SSE swell rapidly filled in Tuesday afternoon and should peak today for the region, before dropping off through the second half of the week as Genevieve tracks to the northwest, very close to the Baja peninsula.
